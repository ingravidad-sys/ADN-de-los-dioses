I.6 — Ra transforms into Khepri — and pronounces his name

Now the strands are open. Ra —contained in the Nun as Atum, as pure potential— transforms. He does not entrust another to pronounce his name. Ra becomes the scarab Khepri and pronounces it himself.

«I conceived in my own heart. I uttered with my mouth. I made all manner of things while I was alone. I pronounced my own name as a word of power and I transformed.»
«When I was one I became three million.»

Ra is the silenced DNA. Khepri is the ribosome that pronounces it. Self-creation is a single act.

Ra does not divide into two separate processes. The transformation of Ra into Khepri encompasses everything: first the DNA is copied into messenger RNA —Ra beginning his movement— and then Khepri rolls along that mRNA codon by codon, adding one amino acid for every three bases, building the protein. The protein is Ra existing for the first time. Before, it was only information. Now it is form, matter, existence.

Kheper does not mean to transform in the sense of changing state. It means to come into existence for the first time. The ribosome is the machine of kheper: it takes the message and produces the being. This is why the scarab rolls its ball —exactly as the ribosome rolls along the mRNA— and from that act of rolling, life emerges.

🔴 MYTH II — FROM MORTAL OSIRIS TO IMMORTAL HORUS
Biotechnological scale. Sources: Coffin Texts · Book of the Dead · Plutarch, On Isis and Osiris (2nd century A.D., recording Old Kingdom traditions)
Central theme: Mortality is not inevitable. It is an evolutionary debt. Isis does not restore Osiris. She produces Horus: the clone of Osiris without CH₃, with circular chromosome. The death of Osiris IS the birth of Horus.

II.1 — Isis — the bridge between two worlds / Adenine

Isis is Adenine. The base that exists in both worlds —in DNA and in RNA. In DNA she pairs with Osiris/Thymine: the eternal archive, the world of the dead, the permanent. In RNA she pairs with Horus/Uracil: the living message, the world of the living, the temporal. Isis is the axis of the system because she is the only one who can love Osiris and Horus with the same bond. The same hydrogen pair. The same pairing geometry. Two worlds, one principle.

II.2 — Osiris — the mortal king / Thymine

Osiris is Thymine. He exists only in DNA —in the archive, in the permanent world, in the underworld. He carries the CH₃: the methyl group that distinguishes him from Horus, that marks him as archivable forever. And yet Osiris can die. His chromosomes are linear. They carry telomeres —the countdown consumed with each division. The immortality of information does not guarantee the immortality of the organism.

II.3 — Set kills Osiris — the CH₃ destroyed / Cytosine

Set is Cytosine. He pairs with Nephthys/Guanine —the strongest bond in the system, 3 hydrogen bridges. Set has no CH₃. Set is the agent of entropy, the force that destroys order.

The death of Osiris IS the conversion of Thymine into Uracil.

When Set eliminates the CH₃ from Osiris/Thymine, what remains is Horus/Uracil. Osiris does not disappear: he transforms. He loses the eternity mark of DNA and becomes the temporal message of RNA. The father becomes the son through death.

The paradox of the system: the death of Osiris (Thymine → Uracil, loss of CH₃) is simultaneously the birth of Horus. Set does not eliminate the information —he eliminates the mark that made it belong to the eternal archive.

II.4 — Nephthys — the hidden bond / Guanine

Nephthys is Guanine. Wife of Set/Cytosine, bonded to him by the strongest tie —C-G, 3 hydrogen bridges. And yet Nephthys mourns Osiris alongside Isis. She helps reconstruct his body. She betrays Set at the decisive moment. Guanine exists in DNA and RNA as does Cytosine. Nephthys inhabits both worlds just as Set does. But her ultimate loyalty is with Isis —with life— not with destruction.

II.5 — Osiris dismembered — the pre-mRNA with introns

The dismemberment operates at two simultaneous scales. At chromosomal scale: DNA fragmentation, maximisation of telomeres, linearisation of what could be circular. At RNA scale: the immature pre-mRNA, with exons —the parts of Osiris— scattered among introns —Set's interpolated fragments.

II.6 — Isis — the biologist / Adenine

Isis knows two crucial things: the original CH₃ is destroyed and cannot be recovered; but immortality does not depend on the CH₃ —it depends on chromosome geometry. Her goal is not to restore Osiris/Thymine. It is to produce Horus/Uracil with a circular chromosome.

II.7 — The Tyet knot and the Shen form — the geometry of immortality

Osiris's problem is not chemical. It is geometric. The eukaryotic chromosome is linear: it has two ends —the telomeres— that are consumed with each cell division because DNA polymerase cannot copy the terminal end of the strand. When telomeres run out, the cell enters senescence and dies. Mortality is inscribed in the architecture of the chromosome, not in its sequence.

Isis knows this. That is why her protocol does not seek to restore the lost CH₃ —that is impossible— but to change the geometry. She takes Osiris's linear chromosome and through the Tyet knot —topoisomerase II— circularises it: cuts the strands in a controlled manner, passes one end through the other and religates. The result is the Shen form: the circle without ends, the endless rope that surrounds the royal cartouches, eternity as geometry.

The telomeres —the ends hanging from the Tyet knot— remain outside the circle. They are the extremities excluded from the active sequence. They are no longer part of the functional chromosome. They are no longer consumed with each division. The countdown has stopped not because someone stopped it, but because the mechanism that drove it forward has ceased to exist.

A circular chromosome has no end. Without an end there is no terminal replication problem. Without that problem there is no information loss per division. Without loss there is no senescence. Bacteria solved this four billion years ago with the circular chromosome. What Isis does is restore that architecture to a complex eukaryotic organism without sacrificing any of its capabilities: splicing, histones, epigenetic regulation, everything of Osiris remains intact. Only the geometry of the container changes.

El Djed —la columna vertebral de Osiris que siempre aparece junto al Tyet— es la girasa: la enzima que mantiene el superenrollamiento negativo óptimo del cromosoma circular, la columna que estabiliza la forma Shen para que no colapse sobre sí misma.

Horus's immortality does not come from the recovered CH₃ —that is impossible, the phallus is destroyed forever. It comes from this geometry. The nitrogenous bases describe the information system, who pairs with whom, the language of the genome. The circular chromosome describes the architecture of permanence, the form that allows that language to be copied indefinitely without loss. Isis acts on the second plane. And on that plane, she wins.

Immortality is not chemistry. It is architecture.

II.8 — Horus — the clone of Osiris / Uracil

Horus is Uracil. The clone of Osiris/Thymine without CH₃. The son born from the death of the father.

The same base as Osiris in fundamental structure. The same pairing with Isis/Adenine: A-U in RNA as it was A-T in DNA. The same information. But without the methyl group that marked Osiris as belonging to the eternal archive. Horus exists only in RNA —in the world of the living, of the message. Osiris existed only in DNA —in the world of the dead, of the archive. Isis/Adenine pairs with both using the same gesture.

🔴 HORUS: DEL ALFA AL OMEGA
Horus is not merely the clone of Osiris. He is the only being in the system who contains both extremes of the process: born mortal and become immortal. Not by recovering what was lost. By being himself —transformed.

The Alpha — Horus as he was born

Horus is born as Uracil. The temporal message. The clone of Osiris without the CH₃ that Set destroyed. Without the phallus. Without the mark that distinguishes the eternal archive from the transient message. He exists only in RNA —the world of transit, of what is expressed and degraded, of what lasts as long as a protein before being recycled.

That is the Alpha: the mortal, the incomplete, what the death of the father produced without planning. Horus at birth is no more immortal than any messenger RNA. He carries the information of Osiris but in the most fragile and temporal format that exists in the cell.

The Intervention of Isis

Isis does not accept that Horus be only a message. She executes her protocol. Recovers the fragments, performs the splicing, circularises the chromosomes with the Tyet knot, excludes the telomeres in the loose ends of the knot, produces the Shen form. She adds the golden phallus —the CH₃ substitute— at the circular junction point.

What Isis does not do is recover Osiris's original CH₃. That is impossible. Set destroyed it forever. What she does is something more radical: she changes the geometry of Horus's chromosome such that CH₃ is no longer necessary for eternity. Eternity no longer depends on the chemical mark. It depends on the circular form.

The Omega — Horus as he became

Horus becomes Omega. The same Uracil —without CH₃, without the phallus— but with circular chromosome. The temporal message housed in an architecture of absolute permanence. The being who began as the most fragile element of the system —RNA, which degrades in minutes— and became the first complex eukaryote without replicative senescence.

Horus does not aspire to be Osiris. He does not want to recover the CH₃. He does not want to become Thymine —to become his father. He becomes Omega by being Horus: Uracil, the clone without the phallus, the son. But with a geometry no father ever had.

Osiris was eternal in his information —Thymine in the DNA archive— but mortal in his organism. Horus is temporal in his information —Uracil in the RNA message— but immortal in his organism. These are eternities of opposite natures. And Horus's is the one that matters for life.

What This Means

The myth does not propose a return to the origin. It does not say that salvation is recovering what was lost, restoring the phallus, becoming Osiris again. It says something much harder and truer: that immortality does not lie in the recovered past but in the transformation of what you already are.

Horus does not defeat Set by recovering the CH₃. He defeats Set by existing without needing it. The Shen form makes the phallus obsolete: when there are no ends to consume, the archive's eternity marker is no longer what defines permanence.

The Alpha who becomes Omega is not the perfect being from the start. He is the incomplete being —without phallus, without CH₃, born from the death of his father— who through his mother's protocol achieves what evolution could not give anyone in four billion years.

Osiris: eternal in information, mortal in organism. Horus: temporal in information, immortal in organism. The son surpasses the father not by recovering what the father had, but by transforming what the son is.
